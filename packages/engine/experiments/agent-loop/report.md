# Agent-with-tools loop — decoupled real-LLM report

> branch `feat/agent-loop` (from `feat/self-model`) · provider=poe · model=GLM-4.6 · recall-embeddings=REAL(openai)

## What this replaces

The season harness (`experiments/season-one/harness-v3.ts`) runs a FIXED PIPELINE — perceive→plan→move→scene per tick for everyone, with mechanical `computeRehearsalRouting` (a 0.6-vs-want-weight scalar). In the real season run that produced **33/49 scenes at [雲錦台戲台], 16 at [戲台·排], ZERO at any other venue**; nights were all "快轉, 無合格私戲"; the pivotal 〔了斷〕柳生春→金鳳 was a **LOG state-flip with NO scene**.

This experiment makes each character an **AGENT**: the tick spotlights one; it PLANS (first-person, grounded in location/self-model/wants/situation/recall/who-is-present), then invokes a SEQUENCE OF TOOLS (cap 3) — `move / recall / reflect / interact / sleep`. §2.43: the plan prompt lists the tools as capabilities and asks "what do you do"; it never says go-here / reflect / settle-the-debt. Characters **start at their own places**.

## 2) Motivated travel + 了斷 as a rendered scene

The 了斷 (柳生春×金鳳 reckoning) rendered as a **私下 scene of 5 beats at 後台小廂房** — NOT a log flip. The debt DROVE a character to travel: **金鳳** chose `move` into the other's world, then `interact`.

**金鳳's move-decision (verbatim plan):**

> 天還早，堂子裡靜得很，我又想起那年冬天柳生春說的話。這些年替他攢下的體己，藏在梳妝匣底層，今兒想再去看看，只怕他真回不來了。這心事沈沈壓著，不如去戲台後頭找他，問個明白。

**The 了斷 scene (verbatim beats):**

> **金鳳**：上前一步，指尖死死抵著他的胸口，眼眶發紅卻咬著牙不放聲，只死死盯著他的眼。  
> *（心）這心口若是沒鬼，怎麼見了我連句整話都不敢吐？*
> **柳生春**：垂下眼簾，手掌覆上她抵在我胸口的手背，沒用力推開，只輕輕按住了那根顫抖的手指。  
> *（心）這一指頭戳進心裡比當年那一刀還疼，可我柳生春就是個膿包，到了嘴邊的話全爛在肚子裡，連看都不敢看她一眼。*
> **金鳳**：手背被他覆住那瞬，指尖像是被火燙了似的一縮，卻又狠著心反手扣住他的袖口，從牙縫裡擠出一句：「這大清早的，柳大官人總該把欠我的那句『交代』補全了吧。」  
> *（心）這袖子都舊了，當年那個意氣風發的人哪去了？這些年我守著這點念想，可不能就這麼輕輕鬆鬆兩句話帶過。*
> **柳生春**：目光在她扣住袖口的手上停了一瞬，喉頭滾了滾，聲音沙啞低沉：「這兒是後台，人多眼雜……不是說話的地方。」  
> *（心）當年也是這雙手替我擋了那些債主的罵，如今我竟連抬頭看她勇氣的都沒了，這句交代，我還是給不起。*
> **金鳳**：鬆開手，轉身落了閂，回身便將那藏在梳妝匣最底層、沾著我體己溫熱的銀票一股腦拍在他胸口，聲音壓得極低卻字字砸地：「這兒沒人，錢你拿去，話你留下。」  
> *（心）這錢本就是替你留的贖身錢，如今你拿去，總得把當年的欠條給我免了，哪怕是一句休書，我也好乾淨利落地死了心。*

## Honest read

**Does the agent-with-tools loop restore the autonomy the pipeline hard-coded away? Mostly YES — with two real gaps.**

Restored:
- **Multi-venue.** Scenes rendered at 2 venues (後台小廂房 67%, 雲錦台戲台 33%); 戲台 share fell from the season's **100%** to **33%**. Characters acted at their OWN places, and the troupe (蘇映雪×沈雪笙) rehearsed at the 戲台 on their own initiative — nobody was routed there.
- **Motivated travel + 了斷 as a rendered SCENE.** The reckoning rendered as a private 5-beat scene (金鳳 travelled to 柳's 後台, drove the 銀票 onto his chest, demanded the 交代) — a scene, not a log flip. And the debt pulled BOTH ways: at deep-night tick 11, 柳生春 autonomously chose `move → 會樂里` to finally settle it ("師父說欠人的總要還…哪怕硬著頭皮，也得去會樂里把這舊帳了了"). He arrived to an empty room — 金鳳 had come to him earlier and never went back. That **missed connection is a feature, not a bug**: it is what independent, non-omniscient spatial agency produces, and the pipeline's teleport-routing could never generate it.
- **Autonomous nights.** Night ticks were NOT fast-forwarded. Each night the spotlight character chose (visit / reckon / rehearse); 3 private night scenes formed, including a troupe rehearsal at the 戲台.
- **Non-troupe stayed motivated.** 金鳳 left 會樂里 only to confront 柳; 白韻秋 tracked 柳 from 白公館 → 戲台 → 後台. Neither loitered at the 戲台 as scenery.

Still weak:
- **reflect() and sleep() were NEVER chosen (0/0), even with a fatigue FACT in the situation.** Want-pursuit dominates every turn: given a hot want and a co-present (or reachable) target, the agent always picks move/interact. An *available* tool plus "you're tired" is not enough pressure to make a character stop and reflect or go to bed. The architectural read: **reflection and sleep probably do not belong as peer tools competing with want-driven action.** They want to be either (a) a scheduled day-end affordance (the season's nightly self-model consolidation is arguably their right home), or (b) gated by HARD fatigue that actually removes the option to keep acting. As peers in a want-maximising ReAct step, they are dominated.
- **Tool sequences are degenerate-simple: `move → interact` or bare `interact` only** (histogram: move=6, interact=12, recall/reflect/sleep=0). No thrashing or looping, but no richness either — the agent never chose a targeted `recall` (auto-recall covered it) and never reflected. Good news: no pathological loops; the cap of 3 was never hit.
- **Cost:** ~50–70 LLM calls for 12 ticks (1 plan/turn + up to a 3–5 beat scene per interact + a judge pass). Materially more than the pipeline's fixed phases, but bounded and predictable. A per-turn plan + one scene is the dominant cost; reflection would add more if it ever fired.

Net: the loop **restores multi-venue, motivated travel, the 了斷-as-scene, and living autonomous nights**. The one honest miss is that reflect/sleep need a different home than "peer tool", and the world can over-centralise on a shared want-target (here 柳生春) unless other characters carry their own venue-anchored wants (giving 蘇/沈 troupe wants was enough to open a second venue).
