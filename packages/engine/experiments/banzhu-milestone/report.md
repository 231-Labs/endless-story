# 班主-milestone experiment — report

- provider: **poe**  ·  mock: **false**
- 本事 gate → contested part(s): **許仙**
- eligibility (本事-gated): 白素貞[蘇映雪]  ·  許仙[柳生春、江聞鶴]  ·  小青[連翹]

## Hypothesis
Route production THROUGH the 班主-as-agent: on her spotlight she decomposes the season goal into the next milestone and ANNOUNCES it in-world; when 定角 comes due she CONVENES the casting scene. Does this make production VISIBLE (a narrated announce→prep→casting→cast→rehearse chain) and pull the cast toward the deadline, vs the silent state machine that read like a green-room?

## 3 · The convened casting scene (verbatim)

班主 **沈雪笙** convened the 選角. Contested part **許仙** — eligible advocates (本事-gated): **柳生春、江聞鶴**.

- **〔爭·柳生春〕** 這《斷橋》裡的許仙，我看就是個沒了魂的空殼子，只剩下一副怕又捨不得的軟骨頭。師姐既然定了調子要那個『悔』字，這種不敢抬頭看人的怯劲儿，我拿捏得住，不費勁。這年底的會串，我就把這摺扇一收，替大家把這點兒『哀而不傷』唱透了，成不成？
  · （心裡）我這演出來的男人，到底能不能留住你？
- **〔爭·蘇映雪〕** 師妹這戲立的是『悔』字，那個悔字不能是光嘴上說說，得從心裡頭往外滲。生春這八年，咱倆在台上那個『眼波才動被人猜』的勁頭，那是假的嗎？他若演個薄倖郎，那是他在台上；今兒要演個回心轉意的軟人，我這白娘子若不把這口氣給他接住了，他那個『悔』字往哪兒擱？這折戲，我來陪他磨，還有誰比我更知他深淺？
  · （心裡）這回我不退了，哪怕就這最後一場，我也想聽你當著全上海的面，再喚我一聲娘子。
- **〔爭·江聞鶴〕** 柳老板這軟骨頭說得輕巧，可這許仙要真成了沒魂的空殼子，那還叫個男人嗎？斷橋這一跪，跪的不是怕，是悔，得跪得硬橋硬馬，把這地磚跪出聲來，才見得那沒說出口的分量。我這紹興土法子雖笨，可這膝蓋是實打實磨出來的，不是擺樣子給台下太太們看的。今兒這會串，我就想跟柳老板台上見個真章，看是那股子怯勁兒抓人，還是這股子硬悔動人。
  · （心裡）我就想看看，在這新上海，到底是你的花樣好看，還是我這老底子扎實。
- **〔爭·連翹〕** 這許仙爭得熱鬧，可這戲裡若沒了小青這口氣，那悔字也就散了。我是京班武行出來的，不懂那些文縐縐的軟語，只知道小青的恨得像把刀，護著姐姐才收了鋒。這折戲我不光要唱，還要借著那股子怒勁兒，亮個相、踢腿、翻個身，讓這斷橋上的風帶著殺氣。沈老板，這小青給我，我保準用這身硬骨頭，把這『哀而不傷』給您立住了。
  · （心裡）我就想站在台心，讓您看看，這上海灘除了風流，還有要命的活法。

## 7 · Honest read

**Does routing production through the 班主-agent make it VISIBLE and pull the cast
toward the premiere? Yes.** Every production beat that was a silent state-machine
transition in season-v3 is now an in-world scene:

- The 班主 decomposed the season goal into three spoken milestones (定角 → 排練 →
  催場), each in her own voice and each carrying a deadline and pressure
  ("紙條壓我茶碗底下"; "炷香不滅…磨不出真東西這碗梨湯誰也別想喝"; "誰也不許出這大門").
- Actors **oriented to the 定角 call mechanically 3/3**: 柳 dodges-but-writes,
  江 kneels硬橋馬 to stake his claim, 蘇 quietly inks a 『白』 and slides it under
  the 班主's teacup — three completely different in-world responses to the *same*
  announcement, none scripted.
- The casting scene **fired as a real convened argument** (the thing that never
  happened in season-v3): 4 advocates argued in-character, 2 本事-eligible
  contenders rebutted, the 班主 arbitrated across all three parts and ruled
  許仙→柳生春 not on bond-strength but on her own secret ("生春那沒魂的空殼子，正合
  我那沒說出口的心事"). This alone is the headline win.
- Post-casting, 2/3 of the cast rehearsed on the 排練 call (§6).

The whole run reads like a troupe working toward a premiere under a clock, not a
green-room of milling actors.

**What is still weak / worth watching:**

1. **The lead can walk off the milestone.** 柳生春, freshly cast as 許仙, slipped
   out toward 金鳳 the moment 排練 was called. Emergent and in-character, but a
   production-critical no-show has no consequence in this harness — the 班主 never
   drags him back, the deadline never bites him personally. A milestone with teeth
   needs per-character stakes / a 班主 follow-up beat.
2. **班主 over-narration risk is low here but present.** Her announcements are
   vivid, but she does 4 of 10 spotlights (0,4,5,9). On a longer season the
   milestone cadence must not crowd out actor turns, or it slides back toward the
   班主 narrating production *at* the cast instead of the cast enacting it.
3. **Style did NOT flatten — it held sharply.** 柳 (摺扇, 嬌軟裹刺, always half an
   eye on 金鳳) vs 江 (跪步, 硬橋硬馬, 紹興土法子 defiance) are unmistakably
   different at every turn (§4). 蘇's 圓場-then-ink-a-single-『白』 and 連's
   槍花-殺氣 are likewise distinct. The 風格 fragment did its job.
4. **Measurement:** the orientation metric is only as good as its lexicon (§6).
   The mechanism outran the pre-registered vocabulary — a good problem, disclosed
   rather than papered over.
